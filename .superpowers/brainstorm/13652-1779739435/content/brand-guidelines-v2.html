07 / Motion

Animation principles

Motion at Excel is purposeful — it guides attention, communicates state, and reinforces quality. Never decorative; always intentional.

Performance

Hover Lift
0.15s · ease · scale 1.03
Buttons and interactive cards lift slightly on hover. Blue shadow intensifies to signal action.
Motion rules
Duration

50–150ms for micro-interactions. 400–600ms for section enters. Never exceed 800ms.

Easing

Use ease-out for enters, ease-in-out for transitions. Avoid linear.

Accessibility

All animations must respect prefers-reduced-motion. Static fallbacks required.

08 / Imagery

Art direction

Photography and graphics at Excel communicate precision and outcomes. Authentic work environments, real data visualisations, no stock-photo clichés.

Primary style
Data-driven environments

Dark workspaces with glowing analytics dashboards. Real metrics, real screens. Deep navy overlays. Shows intelligence in action.

Secondary
Abstract data flows

Blue particle streams and network graphs. Used for hero sections and AI features.

Brand graphic
Blue gradient blocks

Geometric brand graphics for presentations, social media, and email headers.

✓
Use — approved imagery
  • Authentic team environments — real desks, real work, honest lighting
  • Real data dashboards (with client permission and data anonymised)
  • High-contrast, high-clarity editorial photography
  • Geometric, minimal abstract graphics in brand palette
  • Results-forward visuals: before/after charts, growth curves
✕
Avoid — off-brand imagery
  • Generic stock photos of people smiling at laptops
  • Overcrowded, busy compositions with no focal point
  • Warm, lifestyle-forward photography — not our register
  • Clip art, cartoons, or illustrative icons (unless brand-approved)
  • Text-heavy image overlays with poor contrast or legibility

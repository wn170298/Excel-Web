Clear Space Rule

Always maintain a minimum clear space equal to the cap-height of the "X" in the wordmark on all four sides. No graphics, text, or brand elements should enter this zone.

On campaign materials, increase to 2× the X-height for maximum impact. On small applications (favicon, watermark), 0.5× is the minimum acceptable.

07 — Motion & Animation

Motion Principles

Motion at Excel is purposeful and restrained. We animate to guide attention and confirm interactions — never to entertain. Every animation has a job. If it doesn't aid understanding, it doesn't ship.

Fade Up
0.6s · ease · forwards
Primary entrance animation for sections, cards, and hero content. Delay by 100ms per staggered item.
Fade In
0.5s · ease · forwards
Used for overlays, partner logos, and subtle secondary content that appears on scroll.
Card Hover
translateY(−2px) · 0.2s ease
All interactive cards lift 2px on hover. Shadow intensity increases proportionally.
01 /
Respect reduced motion
All animations are wrapped in prefers-reduced-motion — users who opt out see instant state changes with no animation.
02 /
Ease, not linear
All transitions use ease or ease-out. Linear motion feels mechanical. Ease signals intelligence and quality.
03 /
Duration budget: 150–600ms
Micro-interactions (hover, focus) live at 150–200ms. Entrance animations at 500–600ms. Nothing beyond 800ms unless it's a deliberate spotlight effect.
04 /
Stagger for hierarchy
When multiple elements animate in sequence, delay each by 80–120ms. Stagger reveals information flow and teaches the user how to read the layout.
08 — Imagery & Photography

Image Direction

Photography is minimal and intentional. We favour data visualisations, abstract digital textures, and purposeful atmospheric depth over stock photography and generic team shots.

📊
✓ DO
Dark atmospheric backgrounds with subtle grain texture and data-driven graphic overlays. Radial gradients in Excel Blue.
🌈
✕ DON'T
Avoid vibrant multi-colour imagery, rainbow gradients, or highly saturated photography that fights the brand palette.
😊
✕ DON'T
Avoid generic stock photography showing handshakes, smiling teams, or office scenes. They undermine credibility.
Image Rules
🌘
Dark by default
Hero images lean dark. Apply 20–40% dark gradient overlay on any photo used in a banner context to maintain text legibility.
✦
Grain as texture
Apply the SVG grain overlay (opacity 2.8% light, 4% dark) to all hero backgrounds. It adds depth without distraction.
📐
Aspect ratios
Blog thumbnails: 16:9. Profile photos: 1:1 in circles. Hero images: full-bleed. Never crop faces below the chin.

# Excel Homepage Rewrite — Design Specification

## Overview

Rewrite the EXCEL homepage to match PPC Consultancy's persuasive tone and messaging structure while positioning EXCEL uniquely as a **full-stack, AI-powered growth consultancy**. Shift from corporate/feature-focused copy to problem-aware, consultant-style messaging grounded in real client results.

**Scope:** Homepage only (app/[locale]/page.tsx)
**Approach:** Problem-Aware / Consultant Positioning
**Key Data:** Speed Wreckers case study + Auto Point Car Removal referral proof
**Tone:** Direct, confident, genuine, results-focused
**Founder Positioning:** Credibility signal (Option B) — solution-focused first, then reveal Wali as the person behind it

---

## Core Positioning

**Company:** Excel is a full-stack growth consultancy that connects SEO, paid ads, web design, analytics, and automation into one integrated system—powered by AI—for businesses ready to grow predictably.

**Primary Problem Addressed:** Most agencies optimize one channel in isolation. Siloed marketing wastes budget, fragments attribution, and prevents compounding growth.

**Core Solution:** One integrated growth engine that treats marketing as a connected system, not separate competing channels. Every piece of data informs better decisions. AI handles optimization at scale and speed.

**Key Differentiators:**
- Flat-fee pricing (no percentage of spend; incentives aligned)
- Founder-led (Wali works directly with every client; no junior handoffs)
- Full-stack integration (SEO + Paid + Web + Analytics + Automation as one system)
- AI-powered optimization (real machine learning, not buzzword)

## Messaging Tone Guide

**DO:**
- Be direct and specific ("Most agencies..." not "Many businesses...")
- Name real problems (siloed marketing, wasted budget, attribution confusion)
- Back up claims with actual data (Speed Wreckers: 1,092% conversion increase)
- Show genuine founder conviction (Wali's voice: opinionated, accountable, no BS)
- Emphasize integration as the differentiator
- Use confident language without arrogance ("We deliver" not "We're the best")

**DON'T:**
- Use marketing fluff ("unleash your potential," "maximize synergy")
- Make vague promises ("help your business grow")
- Oversell AI as a magic solution
- Minimize service complexity
- Hide behind corporate-speak
- Make claims without proof

**Tone Spectrum:**
- Professional ← → Conversational (land in middle-right: professional but approachable)
- Corporate ← → Consultant (land in middle-right: consultant-focused, not salesy)
- Feature-focused ← → Results-focused (land right: obsess over results)
